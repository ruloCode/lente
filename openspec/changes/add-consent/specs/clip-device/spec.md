# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Recording light
While the hub transcribes the clip's audio, the clip SHALL show a steady, dim light visible to people nearby, distinct from the blinks of a cue, and SHALL keep it dark while paused.

#### Scenario: Paused
- **WHEN** the wearer pauses listening from the phone
- **THEN** the clip's light goes dark within one second
