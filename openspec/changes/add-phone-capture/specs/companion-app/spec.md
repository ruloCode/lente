# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Choose the phone as the device
Dispositivos SHALL offer "Usar el teléfono" and Ahora SHALL offer it whenever no clip is connected; the status row SHALL name the active device (clip or phone).

#### Scenario: Clip missing on Ahora
- **WHEN** Ahora is open and no clip is connected
- **THEN** the page offers to use the phone's microphone and camera with one tap
