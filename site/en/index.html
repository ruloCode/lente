Glasses that help you speak, see and remember

A clip for any pair of glasses and an app. It tells you what to say, describes what is in front of you and remembers what happened. Built on Meta's models.

One Lente, three moments

It hears and sees what you do, remembers what happened and talks back. Every number comes from a measured run in docs/benchmarks.md.

Dilo («say it») · in meetings

One line to say, right on time.

When someone asks you something in your second language, Lente puts a sentence you can say right away on your phone, with its meaning in Spanish underneath. Afterwards you keep the notes, the decisions and the tasks.

3.02 smedian from the end of the question to the card on the phone (n = 17, synthetic eval, 2026-10-28).

Ver («see») · for people who are blind or have low vision

It tells you what is in front of you, when you ask.

A companion that talks. Tap the screen and it describes what the camera sees; ask it «¿qué tengo en la mano?» (what am I holding?) or «¿dónde estoy?» (where am I?) and it answers out loud. It can narrate what changes and stay quiet while it records. It works with TalkBack.

It is a prototype: not a medical or mobility device, and no replacement for your cane, your guide dog or your own judgement.

3.36 smedian from the clip's photo to its description on the phone (n = 35, the real clip with the hub on Modal, 2026-11-04).

Practicar («practice») · at the piano

A patient teacher who hears every note.

The phone hears the note you play, without sending the audio anywhere, and marks each note of the lesson. When you finish, Muse Spark 1.3 gives you one or two sentences of coaching in Lente's voice.

Try it

Sign in with your Google account.

While the hackathon is judged, any Google account can sign in. Your memory is yours alone.

  1. 1

    Sign in

    With Google on the web, or in the Android app with «Iniciar sesión con Google».

  2. 2

    Accept the terms

    The preview terms, once.

  3. 3

    Ask the sample

    Your account starts with «PROBE · Proyecto de ejemplo»: two made-up meetings, tasks and memories. Ask in Spanish or English.

    • ¿Qué se decidió sobre la beta?
    • ¿Qué tareas tengo pendientes?
    • What can you do for me?
  4. 4

    No clip needed

    In the app, «Usar el teléfono» turns the phone's microphone and camera into the clip. On the web, record with your computer's microphone or camera, or a Meet or Zoom tab.

  5. 5

    Delete it whenever you like

    Ajustes → «Borrar ejemplo», or your whole account with all its data.

There is a daily limit per person and one for everyone. If you reach it, Lente tells you, and you can come back the next day.

Install the Android app

  1. On your Android phone, open lente.apk or scan the code.
  2. If Android asks, allow installing apps from that source (your browser or your files app) and tap «Instalar» (Install).
  3. Open Lente and tap «Iniciar sesión con Google».

Android 10 or later. Not on Google Play yet; the notes for each version are on GitHub Releases.

The clip

Build it yourself. It straps to any pair of glasses.

A small board with a camera and a microphone, a battery, and a 3D-printed case.

Parts

  • Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense: camera, microphone and Wi-Fi on one board
  • A one-cell LiPo battery and its USB-C charger
  • A slide switch
  • Optional: a vibration motor for silent cues

Case and firmware

  • Parametric cases with STL files ready to print in hardware/ (v3 in clip-v3/out)
  • The firmware in firmware/lente-clip: audio and photos over Wi-Fi; flash it with arduino-cli
  • A hardware self-test in firmware/selftest

Privacy

Your memory is yours.

The preview terms, in short.

  • It is kept as text, sealed with a key that is yours alone. Audio and photos are kept only for the author's account; every other account keeps text only.

  • You can delete your account at any time, and all your data with it.

  • Lente calls Meta's Model API on free credits, which allow content to be used for product improvement. Don't share anything sensitive.

  • It is a hackathon prototype, not a medical or mobility device.

In short: Terms · Privacy. The text you accept when you sign in is on the hub: terms · privacy. Questions? In the repository's issues.
