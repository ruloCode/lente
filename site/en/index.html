Glasses that help you speak, see and remember

Lente is a clip for any pair of glasses and an app on your phone. It tells you what to say when someone asks you something, describes what is in front of you, and remembers what happened so you can ask about it later. It runs on Meta's models, and it works without the clip too: the phone becomes the microphone and the camera.

The web app works in any browser. The Android app needs Android 10 or later; how to install it. The app speaks Spanish first.

Three uses, one Lente

Lente hears and sees what you do, remembers what happened and talks back. Every number comes from a measured run listed in docs/benchmarks.md.

Dilo («say it»)

In meetings, in your second language

When someone asks you something, Lente puts a sentence you can say right away on your phone, with its meaning in Spanish underneath. Afterwards you keep the notes, the decisions and the tasks.

3.02 s median from the end of the question to the card on the phone (n = 17, synthetic eval, 2026-10-28).

Ver («see»)

For people who are blind or have low vision

A companion that talks. Tap the screen and it tells you what is in front of you; ask it «¿qué tengo en la mano?» (what am I holding?) or «¿dónde estoy?» (where am I?). It can narrate what changes and stay quiet while it records. It is not a medical or mobility device.

3.36 s median from the clip's photo to its description on the phone (n = 35, the real clip with the hub on Modal, 2026-11-04).

Practicar («practice»)

At the piano, at home

The phone hears every note you play, without sending the audio anywhere, and marks each note of the lesson. When you finish, Muse Spark 1.3 gives you one or two sentences of coaching in Lente's voice.

The number arrives with the run at the piano.

Try it now

While the hackathon is judged, any Google account can sign in. Your memory is yours alone.

On the web or on your phone

  1. Sign in with Google on the web, or install the Android app and tap «Iniciar sesión con Google».
  2. Read and accept the preview terms. You do it once.
  3. Your account starts with a sample: the project «PROBE · Proyecto de ejemplo», with two made-up meetings, tasks and memories. Ask it (in Spanish or English):
    • ¿Qué se decidió sobre la beta?
    • ¿Qué tareas tengo pendientes?
    • What can you do for me?
  4. Without the clip: in the app, «Usar el teléfono» turns its microphone and camera into the clip. On the web, record with your computer's microphone or camera, or a Meet or Zoom tab.
  5. Whenever you like, remove the sample in Ajustes → «Borrar ejemplo», or delete your whole account with all its data.

There is a daily limit per person and one for everyone. If you reach it, Lente tells you, and you can come back the next day.

Install the Android app

  1. On your Android phone, open lente.apk or scan the code.
  2. If Android asks, allow installing apps from that source (your browser or your files app) and tap «Instalar» (Install).
  3. Open Lente and tap «Iniciar sesión con Google».

Android 10 or later. The app is not on Google Play yet; the notes for each version are on GitHub Releases.

Build the clip

A small board with a camera and a microphone, a battery, and a 3D-printed case that straps to the temple of any pair of glasses.

Parts

  • Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense: camera, microphone and Wi-Fi on one board.
  • A one-cell LiPo battery and a one-cell USB-C charger.
  • A slide switch.
  • Optional: a vibration motor for silent cues.

Case and firmware

  • Parametric cases in hardware/, with STL files ready to print (v3 in hardware/clip-v3/out) and a 3D viewer.
  • The firmware in firmware/lente-clip: audio and photos over Wi-Fi, with the microphone on its own task so a photo never drops speech. Flash it with arduino-cli.
  • A hardware self-test in firmware/selftest.

Privacy

The preview terms, in short.

  • Lente is a hackathon prototype. It is not a medical or mobility device.
  • Your memory is kept as text, sealed with a key that is yours alone. Audio and photos are kept only for the author's account; every other account keeps text only.
  • Lente calls Meta's Model API on free credits, which allow content to be used for product improvement. Don't share anything sensitive.
  • You can delete your account at any time, and all your data with it.
  • Questions? Write to us in the repository's issues.

In short: Terms · Privacy. The text you accept when you sign in is on the hub: terms · privacy.
