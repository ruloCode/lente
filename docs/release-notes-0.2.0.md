# Lente 0.2.0 (hackathon)

The Android app of Lente, for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. It works with the clip on your glasses, or with the phone alone as the microphone and the camera. Website: <https://rulocode.github.io/lente/> · Web app: <https://drew-lizcano--lente-hub-hub.us-east.modal.run/app>

**Download:** `lente.apk` below, or <https://github.com/ruloCode/lente/releases/latest/download/lente.apk> (always the latest).

## Install

1. **Android 10 or later.**
2. Open `lente.apk` on the phone (from this page, the website's QR code, or your downloads).
3. If Android asks, allow installing apps from that source: *Settings → Install unknown apps → your browser or Files → Allow*. Then tap **Instalar** (Install). Play Protect may say it doesn't know the developer: the app is signed by the author, not distributed through Google Play yet.
4. Already have an earlier Lente? Install this one over it; your account stays in the cloud.

## Sign in

Open Lente and tap **Iniciar sesión con Google**. Accept the preview terms once (a summary, with links to the full terms and the privacy policy). During judging any Google account can sign in, and each one gets its own memory.

## Three things to try first

1. **Ask the sample.** Your account starts with «PROBE · Proyecto de ejemplo»: two made-up meetings, tasks and memories. Ask «¿Qué se decidió sobre la beta?» in the chat or by voice; the answer cites the meeting it came from.
2. **Ver.** Open Ver and let Lente introduce itself. Tap the screen to hear what is in front of the camera, or ask «¿qué tengo en la mano?» or «¿dónde estoy?».
3. **Record a moment.** Without the clip, choose «Usar el teléfono»; then record, talk for a minute, and later ask Lente what was said.

To remove the sample: **Ajustes → Borrar ejemplo**. To delete your account and everything in it: **Ajustes → Borrar mi cuenta**.

## Privacy, in one line

Your memory is kept as text, sealed with a key that is yours alone; audio and photos are kept only for the author's account; the models run on Meta's Model API with free credits that allow content to be used for product improvement, so don't share anything sensitive. The full text is at `/terminos` and `/privacidad` on the hub.

## Known limits

- A hackathon prototype, not a medical or mobility device: don't rely on Ver to get around.
- The app speaks Spanish first; Dilo answers in the language of the question.
- Each account has a daily usage limit, and there is one for everyone; when it is reached, Lente says so and you can come back the next day. New sign-ups close after judging; existing accounts keep working.
- Audio and photos are not kept for your account, only the text.
- The clip's Wi-Fi is set in its firmware; setting it up over Bluetooth is not built yet.
- Not on Google Play yet, so updates come from this page.
