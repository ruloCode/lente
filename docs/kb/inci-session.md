# The INCI session (add-sight-mode, group 7)

A use case recorded with people who are blind or have low vision, at the Instituto Nacional para Ciegos (INCI), Bogotá, on 2026-11-04. This file holds the plan and an empty consent form; the signed forms stay on paper with the owner and are never scanned into the repository.

## INCI

- Carrera 13 # 34-91, Teusaquillo, Bogotá. Monday to Friday, 7:00–16:00.
- Switchboard 601 384 6666; citizen service and WhatsApp 316 874 1014; aciudadano@inci.gov.co. Source: <https://www.inci.gov.co/>, read 2026-11-04.
- Ask for the Centro Cultural or Asistencia Técnica. Say what Lente is, that it is a prototype for a hackathon, that it takes 30–45 minutes, and that nothing is stored.

## Before leaving

- [ ] The phone has the build that passed 4.3, charged; the clip charged; a spare power bank and a USB-C cable.
- [ ] The local hub runs on the laptop; the phone and the clip reach it through the phone's hotspot (test it at home on the hotspot).
- [ ] «Sesión de invitado» on; «Guardar audio» and «Guardar fotos» off; the status line shows «Invitado: no se guarda nada».
- [ ] Two printed consent forms per participant (below), a pen.
- [ ] Test objects: a printed page, the banknotes, a cup.

## On site

1. Read the consent form aloud to each participant, answer questions, get the signature (or a fingerprint and a witness's signature, as INCI usually does). No signature, no recording.
2. Put the clip on their own glasses, or on plain frames you bring.
3. Show only what is built and scored:
   - Ver narrating a room;
   - «léeme este papel»;
   - «¿cuánto es este billete?», only if 5.2 scored 4 of 6 or better;
   - «¿qué tengo en la mano?».
4. Never:
   - ask them to walk, cross or find a door with Lente;
   - point it at medicine for dosage;
   - film anyone who did not sign.
5. Ask one open question on camera: «¿Para qué lo usaría en su día?». Keep their words as they are.

## After

- Footage stays on the owner's disk, outside the repository. Only the edited video is published.
- Turn the guest session off; check that the day and Recuerdos show nothing from the session.
- README row: what was shown and the measured numbers; no names unless a participant asked to be named in writing.
