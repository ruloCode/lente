# Concept images

Illustrations generated during the hackathon (2026-10-26..28) with GPT Image 2.5 through Higgsfield, from our own renders and prompts. They show the direction of the product, **not** finished hardware or software, and are not used in the demo video as if they were built.

| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| `lente-hero.jpg` | The clip on a pair of glasses (from the 3D render) |
| `lente-en-reunion.jpg` | Someone in a video call with a reply card |
| `lente-ui-concepto.jpg` | App concept: before, during and after a meeting |
| `lente-hud-prototipo.jpg`, `lente-hud-pov.jpg` | A possible heads-up display (not built) |
| `lente-ecosistema.jpg` | The ecosystem as an infographic |
| `lente-horizonte.jpg` | A possible future product family |

People in these images are synthetic.

`clip-rig-in-hand.png` is different: a real photo (2026-10-29) of the clip's first bench rig, the Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense with its camera, a 1S USB-C charger and a slide switch, held in the author's hand.

`clip-on-gold-glasses.webp` is a real photo (2026-11-04) of the printed clip held to a pair of gold glasses with two rubber bands. The website's hero, share card (`site/assets/og.jpg`) and clip close-up are cut from it.
